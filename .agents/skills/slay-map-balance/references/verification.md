## CP and comparisons

Follow active Map SOR. Existing formula: p=(W+0.5)/(N+1), CP=1000*ln(1/p)/ln(1/0.7); Wilson95 bounds from W/N transform inversely to CP. Verify eligible N and unresolved-scope rules. Never silently omit unresolved cases to improve a score. Missing entry, unresolved scope or no evidence gives CP null with reason; distinguish an unbounded upper endpoint.

Keep source, difficulty, deck/HP/artifacts, enemies, policies/Predict assumptions, repetitions and actual N with every score. Never equate route CP to one card, pool versions/difficulties, average worker CP, count boss arrivals as wins or extrapolate bots to pupil90/70/50.

Match actual entry/RNG and fixed repeats. Stage2 banks are conditional on survival; missing entry is neither a synthetic healthy entry nor a Stage2 loss. Label survivor bias, repeated seeds and overlapping groups. Survivor upgrades/artifacts do not establish causal Elite reward benefits.
