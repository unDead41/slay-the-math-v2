# Scripted recovery sensitivity

Not a student win-rate study or an engine combat-policy simulation. Starting HP is runtime 60 in all profiles (+actual Ration), not proposed Combat HP 75/60/55.

Moderate cost per Normal/Elite/Boss = 4/8/12 HP. Severe = 7/14/20. Costs are declared assumptions; effects of buffs/card synergy are not translated into damage reductions. A budget depletion stops a case; it is not a measured combat defeat. Recovery includes real positive HP deltas from artifacts/Rest/stage clear/events.
