# Teaching meanings: English and Bahasa Melayu

These scoped explanations supplement the stable artifact-compatible canonical registry.

| Term | Meaning | Maksud mudah | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attack (kategori kad) | Attack is a card category. In a teaching question, use the cards explicitly listed or described as Attack; the label alone supplies no extra rule for the calculation. | Attack ialah kategori kad. Dalam soalan pembelajaran, gunakan kad yang disenaraikan atau diterangkan sebagai Attack; label itu sahaja tidak menambah peraturan pengiraan. | If example cards t21 and t24 are listed as Attack, their Attack set is {t21,t24}. |
| Skill (kategori kad) | Skill names a card category. Read any stated card effect separately; a mathematics question uses only the category, cost or other facts it gives. | Skill ialah nama kategori kad. Baca kesan kad yang dinyatakan secara berasingan; soalan matematik hanya menggunakan kategori, kos atau fakta lain yang diberi. | An example card t23 labelled Skill with a stated cost of 4 Energy belongs to the Skill group. |
| Energy (tenaga) | Energy is the resource used to pay a card’s stated Energy cost. For a calculation, use the amount supplied in the question rather than assuming a starting amount. | Energy ialah sumber untuk membayar kos Energy kad yang dinyatakan. Bagi pengiraan, gunakan jumlah yang diberi dalam soalan tanpa mengandaikan jumlah permulaan. | In a separate example, paying a cost of 4 from 7 Energy leaves 3 Energy. |
| Guard (label kategori kad) | Guard is a printed card-category label in these teaching questions. It is not the combat Defend action. Count the stated Guard cards without assuming damage, block or another effect. | Guard ialah label kategori kad dalam soalan pembelajaran ini, bukannya tindakan Defend dalam pertempuran. Kira kad Guard yang dinyatakan tanpa mengandaikan kesan serangan, pertahanan atau kesan lain. | If example card t25 is labelled Guard, include t25 in the Guard set; the label does not state its combat effect. |
| Fire (tanda bercetak) | Fire is a printed mark used to group the example cards or counters. It is not a new ability. It may overlap with a category such as Attack only as the question states. | Fire ialah tanda bercetak untuk mengumpulkan kad atau pembilang contoh, bukannya kebolehan baharu. Pertindihannya dengan kategori seperti Attack mestilah mengikut maklumat soalan. | An example card t26 may be explicitly labelled Skill and marked Fire; these are two stated descriptions of the same card. |
| Ice (tanda bercetak) | Ice is a printed mark on the example items. Use only the items identified as Ice; do not infer a freezing ability or another game effect. | Ice ialah tanda bercetak pada item contoh. Gunakan item yang dikenal pasti sebagai Ice sahaja tanpa mengandaikan kebolehan membekukan atau kesan permainan lain. | If an example list marks t27 and t30 as Ice, count those two listed cards in the Ice group. |
| Neutral (tanda bercetak) | Neutral is another printed label, not automatically the absence of every label. It is separate from Fire and Ice when the question says each item has exactly one of those marks. | Neutral ialah satu lagi label bercetak, bukan semestinya ketiadaan semua label. Ia berasingan daripada Fire dan Ice apabila soalan menetapkan tepat satu daripada tanda tersebut bagi setiap item. | In an example with one mark per card, a card marked Neutral does not also count as Fire or Ice. |
| Card (kad) | One separate card in the stated example collection. Cards with different IDs are different items, even when their colours or category labels match. | Satu kad yang berasingan dalam koleksi contoh yang diberi. Kad dengan ID berlainan ialah item berlainan walaupun warna atau label kategorinya sama. | Example cards t31 and t32 are two cards even if both have a purple mark. |
| Integer | A number with no fractional part: negative whole numbers, zero and positive whole numbers are integers. | Nombor tanpa bahagian pecahan. Integer merangkumi integer negatif, sifar dan integer positif. | −9, 0 and 14 are integers; 4.5 is not an integer. |
| Positive integer (integer positif) | An integer greater than 0. The list starts at 1, so it excludes zero and negative numbers. | Integer yang lebih besar daripada 0. Senarainya bermula dengan 1, maka sifar dan nombor negatif tidak termasuk. | 23 is a positive integer; 0 and −9 are not positive integers. |
| Nonnegative integer (integer bukan negatif) | An integer that is 0 or greater. Zero is included, and negative numbers are excluded. | Integer yang sama dengan 0 atau lebih besar. Sifar termasuk, manakala nombor negatif tidak termasuk. | A count of 0 or 14 counters is nonnegative; a count of −9 counters is not allowed. |
| Variable (pemboleh ubah) | A symbol, often a letter, that stands for a value. The question tells you which values it may take. | Simbol, biasanya huruf, yang mewakili suatu nilai. Soalan menetapkan nilai yang boleh diwakilinya. | In m + 6 = 23, m represents the number needed before adding 6. |
| Value (nilai) | The number or result represented by a symbol or expression in the stated case. | Nombor atau hasil yang diwakili oleh simbol atau ungkapan bagi kes yang dinyatakan. | When m = 9, the value of m + 6 is 15. |
| Factor (faktor) | A nonzero integer that divides another integer exactly, leaving no remainder. Use positive factors when the question asks for positive factors. | Integer bukan sifar yang membahagi integer lain dengan tepat tanpa baki. Gunakan faktor positif apabila soalan meminta faktor positif. | 7 is a factor of 56 because 56 ÷ 7 = 8 with no remainder. |
| Multiple (gandaan) | A number obtained by multiplying the stated number by an integer. Follow the question’s range when listing multiples. | Nombor yang diperoleh dengan mendarab nombor yang dinyatakan dengan integer. Ikut julat soalan apabila menyenaraikan gandaan. | 66 is a multiple of 11 because 11 × 6 = 66. |
| Divisible by (boleh dibahagi tepat dengan) | Can be divided by the stated nonzero integer with an integer result and no remainder. | Boleh dibahagi dengan integer bukan sifar yang dinyatakan sehingga hasilnya integer tanpa baki. | 78 is divisible by 6 because 78 ÷ 6 = 13. |
| Even number (nombor genap) | An integer divisible by 2 without a remainder. Zero and negative multiples of 2 are also even. | Integer yang boleh dibahagi tepat dengan 2 tanpa baki. Sifar dan gandaan negatif bagi 2 juga ialah nombor genap. | −18 and 26 are even: each is 2 times an integer. |
| Odd number (nombor ganjil) | An integer that is not divisible by 2 without a remainder. Odd integers can be positive or negative. | Integer yang tidak boleh dibahagi tepat dengan 2 tanpa baki. Integer ganjil boleh positif atau negatif. | −15 and 23 are odd; 0 is even. |
| Prime number (nombor perdana) | An integer greater than 1 with exactly two positive factors: 1 and itself. The number 1 is not prime. | Integer yang lebih besar daripada 1 dan mempunyai tepat dua faktor positif: 1 dan dirinya sendiri. Nombor 1 bukan nombor perdana. | 19 is prime because its only positive factors are 1 and 19. |
| Square / n² (kuasa dua) | To square a number, multiply it by itself. The raised 2 in n² means n × n, not 2 × n. | Untuk mendapatkan kuasa dua sesuatu nombor, darabkan nombor itu dengan dirinya sendiri. Angka kecil 2 dalam n² bermaksud n × n, bukannya 2 × n. | 9² = 9 × 9 = 81, whereas 2 × 9 = 18. |
| Sum / total (hasil tambah / jumlah) | The result of adding numbers or counts. | Hasil apabila nombor atau bilangan ditambah. | The sum of 11 and 14 is 11 + 14 = 25. |
| Product / multiply (hasil darab / darab) | Multiply means use the × operation. The result of that operation is the product. | Darab bermaksud menggunakan operasi ×. Hasil operasi itu dipanggil hasil darab. | The product of 6 and 9 is 6 × 9 = 54. |
| Fraction (pecahan) | A number written as a numerator (top number) divided by a denominator (bottom number), which cannot be zero. For equal parts of a whole, the numerator counts chosen parts and the denominator counts all parts. | Nombor yang ditulis sebagai pengangka (nombor di atas) dibahagi dengan penyebut (nombor di bawah), yang tidak boleh sifar. Bagi bahagian sama besar, pengangka mengira bahagian dipilih dan penyebut mengira semua bahagian. | 5/11 can describe 5 chosen parts out of 11 equal parts. |
| Denominator / common denominator (penyebut / penyebut sepunya) | A denominator is the bottom number in a fraction. A common denominator is the same bottom number used to rewrite two or more fractions in equal-sized parts before adding, subtracting or comparing them. | Penyebut ialah nombor di bawah garis pecahan. Penyebut sepunya ialah penyebut sama yang digunakan untuk menulis semula dua atau lebih pecahan dalam bahagian sama besar sebelum ditambah, ditolak atau dibandingkan. | In 5/11, the denominator is 11. To compare 2/3 and 1/7, use the common denominator 21: 14/21 and 3/21. |
| Remainder (baki) | The amount left after forming as many complete equal groups as possible in a whole-number division. | Jumlah yang tinggal selepas membentuk sebanyak mungkin kumpulan lengkap yang sama besar dalam pembahagian nombor bulat. | 29 ÷ 6 gives 4 complete groups with remainder 5, since 29 = 6 × 4 + 5. |
| Greater than / > (lebih besar daripada) | Larger than the stated boundary. The boundary value itself is excluded. | Lebih besar daripada nilai sempadan yang dinyatakan. Nilai sempadan itu sendiri tidak termasuk. | x > 11 allows 12 but excludes 11. |
| Less than / < (lebih kecil daripada) | Smaller than the stated boundary. The boundary value itself is excluded. | Lebih kecil daripada nilai sempadan yang dinyatakan. Nilai sempadan itu sendiri tidak termasuk. | x < 11 allows 10 but excludes 11. |
| Greater than or equal to / ≥ (lebih besar daripada atau sama dengan) | The boundary value or any larger allowed value. The equals part includes the boundary. | Nilai sempadan atau mana-mana nilai lebih besar yang dibenarkan. Bahagian sama dengan memasukkan nilai sempadan. | x ≥ 11 includes 11 and 12. |
| Less than or equal to / ≤ (lebih kecil daripada atau sama dengan) | The boundary value or any smaller allowed value. The equals part includes the boundary. | Nilai sempadan atau mana-mana nilai lebih kecil yang dibenarkan. Bahagian sama dengan memasukkan nilai sempadan. | x ≤ 11 includes 11 and 10. |
| At least (sekurang-kurangnya) | The stated count is the minimum. Larger counts are included up to the maximum possible in the question. | Bilangan yang dinyatakan ialah minimum. Bilangan yang lebih besar turut termasuk sehingga maksimum yang mungkin dalam soalan. | With 6 example cards, at least 4 marked cards means 4, 5 or 6 marked cards. |
| At most (paling banyak) | The stated count is the maximum. Smaller allowed counts, including zero when possible, are included. | Bilangan yang dinyatakan ialah maksimum. Bilangan lebih kecil yang dibenarkan turut termasuk, termasuk sifar jika mungkin. | At most 4 selected counters allows 0, 1, 2, 3 or 4 counters. |
| Exactly (tepat) | The stated count only: neither more nor fewer. | Bilangan yang dinyatakan sahaja: tidak lebih dan tidak kurang. | Exactly 4 marked cards means 4; it excludes 3 and 5. |
| Exactly one (tepat satu) | Only one item or condition qualifies. For two conditions, one is true and the other is false; both together are excluded. | Hanya satu item atau syarat memenuhi kehendak. Bagi dua syarat, satu benar dan satu lagi palsu; kedua-duanya benar tidak termasuk. | Exactly one of two example cards has a circle mark: first only or second only, but not both. |
| Ellipsis / ... (titik berturutan) | The dots continue the displayed pattern. Stop at the stated endpoint; do not add values beyond it. | Titik itu meneruskan pola yang dipaparkan. Berhenti pada nilai akhir yang diberi tanpa menambah nilai selepasnya. | {7,14,...,35} means {7,14,21,28,35}. |
| In that order (mengikut turutan itu) | Record results in the same order as the named items. Swapping their positions changes what the answer says. | Rekodkan keputusan mengikut urutan perkara yang disebut. Menukar kedudukan mengubah maksud jawapan. | For “cost, then number of cards”, the pair (4,9) means cost 4 and 9 cards. |
| Probability / P(A) (kebarangkalian) | A measure of an event’s chance from 0 to 1, including both endpoints. For equally likely outcomes, divide the number matching the event by the number of all outcomes. | Ukuran peluang sesuatu peristiwa dari 0 hingga 1, termasuk kedua-dua hujung. Bagi kesudahan sama boleh jadi, bahagikan bilangan yang memenuhi peristiwa dengan bilangan semua kesudahan. | In a separate bag with 3 purple and 8 white counters, one random draw gives P(purple) = 3/11. |
| Experiment / trial (eksperimen / percubaan) | The stated process that produces a result. One trial can include several stages when the question defines it that way. | Proses yang ditetapkan untuk menghasilkan kesudahan. Satu percubaan boleh merangkumi beberapa peringkat jika soalan mentakrifkannya begitu. | “Draw one counter, then spin a numbered wheel once” is a two-stage experiment. |
| Outcome (kesudahan) | One possible complete result of the experiment. Include every stage the question asks you to record. | Satu keputusan lengkap yang mungkin bagi eksperimen. Masukkan setiap peringkat yang perlu direkodkan menurut soalan. | For a counter draw followed by a numbered spin, (purple, 7) is one possible outcome if those results are allowed. |
| Ordered pair / ordered outcome (pasangan tertib / kesudahan berturutan) | A result with labelled positions, such as first draw and second draw. Two unequal entries exchanged between positions give a different outcome. | Kesudahan yang mempunyai kedudukan tertentu, seperti cabutan pertama dan kedua. Menukar kedudukan dua unsur yang berlainan menghasilkan kesudahan berlainan. | For two draws, (t31,t32) and (t32,t31) record different orders. |
| Sample space (ruang sampel) | The set of every possible complete outcome of the stated experiment. Include each distinct outcome once. | Set semua kesudahan lengkap yang mungkin bagi eksperimen yang dinyatakan. Masukkan setiap kesudahan berbeza sekali sahaja. | If one counter is drawn from a bag containing only t31, t32 and t33, the sample space is {t31,t32,t33}. |
| Favourable / matching outcomes (kesudahan yang memenuhi peristiwa) | Outcomes that meet the condition being asked for. “Favourable” means matching the event, not necessarily beneficial to the player. | Kesudahan yang memenuhi syarat yang ditanya. “Favourable” bermaksud memenuhi peristiwa, bukan semestinya menguntungkan pemain. | If a numbered spin from 1 to 11 must exceed 7, the matching outcomes are 8, 9, 10 and 11. |
| Fair (adil) | Each basic result of the stated device has the same chance. Fairness alone does not mean different events, such as dice totals, all have the same probability. | Setiap kesudahan asas alat yang dinyatakan mempunyai peluang sama. Sifat adil sahaja tidak bermaksud semua peristiwa, seperti jumlah dua dadu, mempunyai kebarangkalian sama. | A fair spinner with 7 equal numbered sectors gives each sector probability 1/7. |
| Uniform draw (cabutan dengan peluang sama bagi setiap item) | Every individual item currently available has the same chance of being drawn. A colour with more items can still be more likely than another colour. | Setiap item yang tersedia mempunyai peluang sama untuk dicabut. Warna yang mempunyai lebih banyak item masih boleh lebih mungkin dipilih daripada warna lain. | With 11 separate cards available, a uniform draw gives each particular card probability 1/11. |
| Equally likely (sama boleh jadi) | The outcomes being compared have the same probability. Check which outcomes are being treated as equal before using a simple counting fraction. | Kesudahan yang dibandingkan mempunyai kebarangkalian sama. Semak kesudahan yang dianggap sama peluang sebelum menggunakan pecahan berdasarkan bilangan. | For a uniform draw from 11 separate cards, the 11 individual-card outcomes are equally likely. |
| Event (peristiwa) | A set of outcomes satisfying a stated condition. An event may contain one, several or no outcomes. | Set kesudahan yang memenuhi syarat tertentu. Sesuatu peristiwa boleh mengandungi satu, beberapa atau tiada kesudahan. | From numbered cards 1 to 11, the event “number greater than 8” is {9,10,11}. |
| Independent events (peristiwa tak bersandar) | Learning that one event happened does not change the other event’s probability. For independent events, P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B). | Mengetahui satu peristiwa berlaku tidak mengubah kebarangkalian peristiwa yang satu lagi. Bagi peristiwa tak bersandar, P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B). | In a separate 3-purple, 8-white bag, returning and mixing after each draw keeps the next purple chance at 3/11 whatever the first colour. |
| Dependent events (peristiwa bersandar) | Knowing that one event happened changes the probability of the other. Use the updated situation when calculating the later chance. | Mengetahui satu peristiwa berlaku mengubah kebarangkalian peristiwa yang satu lagi. Gunakan keadaan yang dikemas kini untuk mengira peluang seterusnya. | From a separate 3-purple, 8-white bag, keeping a first purple out changes the next purple fraction from 3/11 to 2/10. |
| With replacement (dengan pemulangan) | Return the drawn item and mix before drawing again. This restores the original collection for the next uniform draw. | Masukkan semula item yang dicabut dan campur sebelum cabutan berikutnya. Koleksi asal dipulihkan untuk cabutan seragam seterusnya. | An example bag with 11 counters still has all 11 available after the first drawn counter is returned. |
| Without replacement (tanpa pemulangan) | Keep each drawn item out. Recount the remaining items and total before the next draw. | Jangan masukkan semula item yang dicabut. Kira semula item yang tinggal dan jumlahnya sebelum cabutan berikutnya. | After one counter is kept out of an 11-counter bag, 10 counters remain for the next draw. |
| Mutually exclusive / disjoint (saling eksklusif / tidak bertindih) | The two events cannot both occur in the same experiment because they share no outcome. Disjoint sets likewise share no element. | Dua peristiwa tidak boleh berlaku bersama dalam eksperimen yang sama kerana tiada kesudahan dikongsi. Set yang tidak bertindih juga tidak berkongsi unsur. | If each example card has exactly one colour, “purple” and “yellow” are mutually exclusive for one card selection. |
| Overlap (pertindihan) | The elements or outcomes shared by the groups. A shared item belongs to each relevant group but is counted only once in their union. | Unsur atau kesudahan yang dikongsi oleh kumpulan. Item yang dikongsi berada dalam setiap kumpulan berkenaan tetapi dikira sekali sahaja dalam kesatuannya. | For A = {21,25} and B = {25,29}, the overlap contains 25. |
| In either order (dalam mana-mana turutan) | Both orders of the stated two results are accepted. Calculate or list each order separately before combining them. | Kedua-dua turutan bagi dua keputusan yang dinyatakan diterima. Kira atau senaraikan setiap turutan secara berasingan sebelum menggabungkannya. | One purple and one white means purple-white or white-purple when either order is allowed. |
| Heads / Tails / H / T (dua sisi syiling) | H means Heads and T means Tails, the two named sides of the coin. Keep these letters as defined in the question when recording toss order. | H mewakili Heads dan T mewakili Tails, iaitu dua sisi syiling yang dinamakan dalam soalan. Kekalkan maksud huruf yang diberi apabila merekodkan turutan lambungan. | THTH records Tails, Heads, Tails, Heads in four successive tosses. |
| Die / dice (dadu) | A die is one rollable object with labelled faces; dice is the plural. Use the face labels and face count stated in the question. | Die merujuk kepada sebiji dadu dengan muka berlabel; dice ialah bentuk jamaknya dalam bahasa Inggeris. Gunakan label dan bilangan muka yang dinyatakan. | If an example specifies a die numbered 1 to 10, its possible face results are 1 through 10. |
| Conditional probability (kebarangkalian bersyarat) | The chance of A after you know B has occurred. P(A \| B) means probability of A given B: count only the outcomes in B when forming the new fraction. | Peluang A apabila B diketahui telah berlaku. P(A \| B) bermaksud kebarangkalian A diberi B: gunakan kesudahan dalam B sahaja untuk membentuk pecahan baharu. | If 4 of 11 striped example counters are purple, P(purple \| striped) = 4/11; unstriped counters are left out of this comparison. |
| Set (set) | A collection of distinct elements. Repeating an already listed element does not create another element. | Kumpulan unsur yang berbeza. Mengulang unsur yang telah disenaraikan tidak menghasilkan unsur tambahan. | {21,25,29} is a set of three numbers; writing 25 twice does not increase its size. |
| Element (unsur) | One member of a set. It may be a number, a name, a symbol or a separate card ID. | Satu ahli sesuatu set. Unsur boleh berupa nombor, nama, simbol atau ID kad yang berasingan. | 25 is an element of {21,25,29}. |
| Belongs to / membership (keahlian set) | ∈ means “is an element of”; ∉ means “is not an element of”. Check whether the item appears in the stated set or satisfies its defining condition. | ∈ bermaksud “ialah unsur bagi”; ∉ bermaksud “bukan unsur bagi”. Semak sama ada item berada dalam set yang diberi atau memenuhi syarat takrifnya. | 25 ∈ {21,25,29}, but 31 ∉ {21,25,29}. |
| Universal set / U (set semesta) | The complete collection of elements allowed in the question. A set complement includes only elements from this same universal set. | Koleksi lengkap unsur yang dibenarkan dalam soalan. Pelengkap set hanya melibatkan unsur daripada set semesta yang sama. | If U = {21,25,29}, no complement in U can introduce 31. |
| Finite set (set terhingga) | A set with a finite number of elements, so its elements can all be counted and the counting ends. | Set yang mempunyai bilangan unsur terhingga, maka semua unsurnya boleh dikira sehingga selesai. | The set of example card IDs {t31,t32,t33,t34} is finite. |
| Subset / ⊆ (subset) | A ⊆ B means every element of A also belongs to B. Equality is allowed: a set is a subset of itself. | A ⊆ B bermaksud setiap unsur A juga berada dalam B. Kedua-dua set boleh sama: sesuatu set ialah subset kepada dirinya sendiri. | {21,29} ⊆ {21,25,29} because both elements on the left occur on the right. |
| Intersection / ∩ (persilangan) | The set of elements shared by every set being intersected. An element must pass all the named membership conditions. | Set unsur yang dikongsi oleh semua set yang dipersilangkan. Sesuatu unsur mesti memenuhi semua syarat keahlian yang dinamakan. | If A = {21,25} and B = {25,29}, A ∩ B = {25}. |
| Union / ∪ (kesatuan) | The set of elements in at least one of the named sets. Include shared elements too, writing each distinct element once. | Set unsur dalam sekurang-kurangnya satu set yang dinamakan. Masukkan unsur yang dikongsi juga, dengan setiap unsur berbeza ditulis sekali sahaja. | If A = {21,25} and B = {25,29}, A ∪ B = {21,25,29}. |
| Complement / A′ (pelengkap) | A′ contains every element of the universal set U that is not in A. For a bracketed expression, the prime applies to the whole set inside the brackets. | A′ mengandungi semua unsur dalam set semesta U yang tidak berada dalam A. Bagi ungkapan berkurung, tanda prima merujuk kepada keseluruhan set di dalam kurungan. | If U = {21,25,29,31} and A = {21,29}, then A′ = {25,31}. |
| Empty set / ∅ (set kosong) | A set with no elements, so its number of elements is 0. It differs from {0}, which contains one element: the number zero. | Set yang tidak mempunyai unsur, maka bilangan unsurnya 0. Ia berbeza daripada {0}, yang mengandungi satu unsur, iaitu nombor sifar. | If A = {21} and B = {25}, then A ∩ B = ∅. |
| Number of elements / n(A) (bilangan unsur) | n(A) counts the different elements of A. It is a number, whereas A itself is the set or list of elements. | n(A) mengira unsur berbeza dalam A. Ia ialah nombor, manakala A sendiri ialah set atau senarai unsur. | For A = {21,25,29,31}, n(A) = 4; the answer to n(A) is the count, not the listed set. |
| Brackets / parentheses (kurungan) | Brackets group an expression so that the operation inside is handled before an outside operation. Check the whole bracketed group when applying a complement. | Kurungan mengumpulkan ungkapan supaya operasi di dalamnya dilakukan sebelum operasi di luar. Pertimbangkan seluruh kumpulan berkurung apabila mengambil pelengkap. | In 3 × (8 + 5), add inside the brackets first: 3 × 13 = 39. |
| Inclusion-exclusion (membetulkan kiraan bertindih) | Add group counts and then correct repeated counting. For two groups, subtract their shared count once. For three groups, subtract the three pairwise counts, then add the all-three count once. | Tambah bilangan kumpulan kemudian betulkan kiraan berulang. Bagi dua kumpulan, tolak bilangan yang dikongsi sekali. Bagi tiga kumpulan, tolak tiga persilangan berpasangan kemudian tambah persilangan ketiga-tiganya sekali. | In a separate collection, 17 circle-marked cards and 12 striped cards share 6 cards; at least one mark covers 17 + 12 − 6 = 23 cards. |
| Pairwise / triple intersection (persilangan berpasangan / tiga set) | Pairwise means two sets at a time. A triple intersection belongs to all three sets. A pairwise intersection includes the all-three elements unless the question explicitly says “only”. | Berpasangan bermaksud dua set pada satu masa. Persilangan tiga set mengandungi unsur dalam ketiga-tiga set. Persilangan berpasangan termasuk unsur ketiga-tiganya kecuali soalan menyebut “sahaja”. | If n(A ∩ B) = 7 and 3 of those elements also belong to C, then 7 − 3 = 4 belong to A and B but not C. |
| De Morgan’s law (hukum De Morgan) | To negate “both”, say at least one part fails. To negate inclusive “or”, say both parts fail. For sets, complement both sets and exchange ∩ with ∪. | Untuk menafikan “kedua-duanya”, nyatakan sekurang-kurangnya satu bahagian gagal. Untuk menafikan “atau” inklusif, nyatakan kedua-duanya gagal. Bagi set, ambil pelengkap kedua-dua set dan tukar ∩ dengan ∪. | “Not (purple or striped)” means “not purple and not striped”. In symbols, (A ∪ B)′ = A′ ∩ B′. |
| Statement (pernyataan) | A sentence with a definite truth value: true or false, but not both. A false mathematical claim is still a statement. | Ayat yang mempunyai nilai kebenaran tertentu: benar atau palsu, tetapi bukan kedua-duanya. Dakwaan matematik yang palsu juga ialah pernyataan. | “23 + 6 = 29” is a true statement; “23 + 6 = 30” is a false statement. |
| Truth value (nilai kebenaran) | Whether a statement is true or false in the stated situation. Work out each simple claim before evaluating a statement that combines claims. | Status benar atau palsu sesuatu pernyataan dalam keadaan yang diberi. Tentukan setiap dakwaan ringkas sebelum menilai gabungannya. | “26 is even” has truth value True; “26 is odd” has truth value False. |
| True (benar) | The claim agrees with the stated facts or correct calculation. | Dakwaan sepadan dengan fakta yang diberi atau pengiraan yang betul. | “11 + 14 = 25” is true. |
| False (palsu) | The claim disagrees with the stated facts or correct calculation. A false claim may still be a statement. | Dakwaan tidak sepadan dengan fakta yang diberi atau pengiraan yang betul. Dakwaan palsu masih boleh menjadi pernyataan. | “11 + 14 = 26” is false because the sum is 25. |
| Open sentence (ayat terbuka) | A claim with an unspecified variable whose truth depends on its value. Supplying a value or a suitable quantifier can make the claim definite. | Dakwaan dengan pemboleh ubah yang belum ditetapkan dan kebenarannya bergantung pada nilai itu. Menetapkan nilai atau pengkuantiti yang sesuai boleh menjadikan dakwaan itu tertentu. | “m + 6 = 23” is open while m is unspecified; different values of m can make it true or false. |
| Quantifier (pengkuantiti) | A word describing how many allowed cases a claim covers. “All” covers every case; “some” means at least one and may include all. | Perkataan yang menerangkan berapa banyak kes yang diliputi dakwaan. “Semua” meliputi setiap kes; “sebilangan” bermaksud sekurang-kurangnya satu dan boleh meliputi semuanya. | “All 6 example cards are striped” checks every card; “some are striped” needs at least one striped card. |
| All / every (semua / setiap) | Every member in the stated domain must satisfy the claim. A single allowed exception disproves a claim about all members. | Setiap ahli dalam domain yang diberi mesti memenuhi dakwaan. Satu pengecualian yang dibenarkan sudah menolak dakwaan tentang semua ahli. | If 5 of 6 example cards are striped and one is plain, “all 6 are striped” is false. |
| Some (sebilangan / sekurang-kurangnya satu) | At least one allowed example satisfies the claim. This may be one, several or all; it does not require another example to fail. | Sekurang-kurangnya satu contoh yang dibenarkan memenuhi dakwaan. Ini boleh melibatkan satu, beberapa atau semuanya; tidak perlu ada contoh lain yang gagal. | If all 6 example cards are striped, “some cards are striped” is still true because at least one exists. |
| None / no (tiada satu pun) | No allowed item satisfies the condition. The number of matching items is zero. | Tiada satu pun item yang dibenarkan memenuhi syarat. Bilangan item yang memenuhi syarat ialah sifar. | “No example card is striped” means the collection has 0 striped cards. |
| Neither ... nor (bukan A dan bukan B) | The item belongs to neither group: it is not in the first and not in the second. It lies outside their union. | Item tidak berada dalam mana-mana daripada dua kumpulan: bukan dalam kumpulan pertama dan bukan dalam kumpulan kedua. Item berada di luar kesatuan kedua-duanya. | A plain yellow example card is neither purple nor striped: both descriptions fail. |
| Domain (domain / nilai yang dibenarkan) | The values or objects allowed for the variable or claim. A test example or counterexample must come from this domain. | Nilai atau objek yang dibenarkan bagi pemboleh ubah atau dakwaan. Contoh ujian atau contoh penyangkal mesti datang daripada domain ini. | If D = {11,13,17}, a test value of 19 is outside the domain. |
| Compound statement (pernyataan majmuk) | A statement formed by combining simpler statements, for example with AND or OR. Determine each part’s truth value before combining them. | Pernyataan yang dibentuk dengan menggabungkan pernyataan ringkas, contohnya menggunakan “dan” atau “atau”. Tentukan nilai kebenaran setiap bahagian sebelum menggabungkannya. | “26 is even AND 19 is prime” joins two mathematical statements. |
| AND (dan) | Both parts must hold in the same case. For truth values, an AND statement is true only when both parts are true. | Kedua-dua bahagian mesti dipenuhi dalam kes yang sama. Bagi nilai kebenaran, pernyataan DAN benar hanya apabila kedua-dua bahagian benar. | An example card is accepted by “purple AND cost 4” only if that same card is purple and has stated cost 4. |
| OR / inclusive OR (atau inklusif) | At least one part must hold. Both parts may hold too; inclusive OR does not mean exactly one. | Sekurang-kurangnya satu bahagian mesti dipenuhi. Kedua-duanya juga boleh dipenuhi; ATAU inklusif tidak bermaksud tepat satu. | “Purple OR striped” accepts a purple card, a striped card, and a card that is both purple and striped. |
| NOT / negation (bukan / tidak / penafian) | A statement’s negation is true exactly when the original is false, and false when the original is true. Cover every excluded case, including boundary values. | Penafian benar tepat apabila pernyataan asal palsu, dan palsu apabila pernyataan asal benar. Liputi semua kes yang tidak termasuk, termasuk nilai sempadan. | The negation of “m > 11” is “m ≤ 11”, which includes m = 11. |
| Implication / if p then q (implikasi / jika p maka q) | A one-way if-then statement. It is false only when p is true and q is false. It does not automatically assert the reverse direction. | Pernyataan jika-maka satu arah. Ia palsu hanya apabila p benar dan q palsu. Pernyataan itu tidak memberikan arah akas secara automatik. | “If n is a multiple of 14, then n is a multiple of 7” gives a rule from multiples of 14 to multiples of 7. |
| If and only if / biconditional (jika dan hanya jika) | Both directions are asserted: if p then q, and if q then p. A case that breaks either direction disproves the whole claim. | Kedua-dua arah dinyatakan: jika p maka q, dan jika q maka p. Kes yang menggagalkan mana-mana arah menolak keseluruhan dakwaan. | For whole numbers, “n ends in digit 0 if and only if n is divisible by 10” includes both directions. |
| Premise (premis) | A starting statement used to support an argument’s conclusion. An if-then rule can be a premise; the part after “if” is its condition, called the antecedent. | Pernyataan awal yang digunakan untuk menyokong kesimpulan hujah. Peraturan jika-maka boleh menjadi premis; bahagian selepas “jika” ialah syaratnya, yang dipanggil antejadian. | “All multiples of 14 are multiples of 7” and “42 is a multiple of 14” can be two premises. |
| Conclusion (kesimpulan) | The statement an argument claims follows from its premises. It must be justified by those premises, not added as another assumption. | Pernyataan yang dikatakan mengikuti premis hujah. Ia mesti dijustifikasikan daripada premis tersebut, bukan ditambah sebagai andaian lain. | From “all multiples of 14 are multiples of 7” and “42 is a multiple of 14”, conclude “42 is a multiple of 7”. |
| Converse (akas) | The converse of “if p then q” is “if q then p”. Swap the two parts without negating them. A true original statement can have a false converse. | Akas bagi “jika p maka q” ialah “jika q maka p”. Tukar kedudukan kedua-dua bahagian tanpa menafikannya. Pernyataan asal yang benar boleh mempunyai akas yang palsu. | The converse of “if n is a multiple of 14, then n is a multiple of 7” is “if n is a multiple of 7, then n is a multiple of 14”. |
| Inverse (songsangan) | The inverse of “if p then q” is “if not p then not q”. Negate both parts while keeping their original order; it need not share the original truth value. | Songsangan bagi “jika p maka q” ialah “jika bukan p maka bukan q”. Nafikan kedua-dua bahagian dengan mengekalkan turutan asal; nilai kebenarannya mungkin berbeza daripada pernyataan asal. | Start with “if n is a multiple of 14, then n is a multiple of 7”. Its inverse is “if n is not a multiple of 14, then n is not a multiple of 7”. |
| Contrapositive (kontrapositif) | The contrapositive of “if p then q” is “if not q then not p”. Reverse the order and negate both parts. It is logically equivalent to the original. | Kontrapositif bagi “jika p maka q” ialah “jika bukan q maka bukan p”. Tukar turutan dan nafikan kedua-dua bahagian. Kontrapositif itu setara secara logik dengan pernyataan asal. | Start with “if n is a multiple of 14, then n is a multiple of 7”. Its contrapositive is “if n is not a multiple of 7, then n is not a multiple of 14”. |
| Logically equivalent (setara secara logik) | Two statements are logically equivalent when their truth values match in every allowed case, not just in one example. | Dua pernyataan setara secara logik apabila nilai kebenarannya sama bagi setiap kes yang dibenarkan, bukan untuk satu contoh sahaja. | “m ≤ 11” and “not (m > 11)” have the same truth value for every real value of m. |
| Truth table (jadual kebenaran) | A table of all possible truth-value combinations for the simple claims. It helps compare complete logical expressions one case at a time. | Jadual semua gabungan nilai kebenaran yang mungkin bagi dakwaan ringkas. Jadual ini membantu membandingkan ungkapan logik lengkap satu kes pada satu masa. | For two unrestricted claims p and q, list (True,True), (True,False), (False,True) and (False,False). |
| Counterexample (contoh penyangkal) | An example inside the allowed domain that makes a general claim false. To refute “if p then q”, the example must satisfy p and fail q. | Contoh dalam domain yang dibenarkan yang menjadikan dakwaan umum palsu. Untuk menolak “jika p maka q”, contoh mesti memenuhi p tetapi tidak memenuhi q. | 18 disproves “every multiple of 6 is a multiple of 12”: it is a multiple of 6 but not of 12. |
| Argument (hujah) | One or more premises together with a conclusion that is claimed to follow from them. | Satu atau lebih premis bersama kesimpulan yang dikatakan mengikuti premis tersebut. | “All multiples of 14 are multiples of 7; 42 is a multiple of 14; therefore 42 is a multiple of 7” is an argument. |
| Valid argument (hujah sah) | An argument is valid when true premises cannot lead to a false conclusion. This checks the reasoning form, separately from whether its stated premises are actually true. | Hujah sah apabila premis benar tidak boleh menghasilkan kesimpulan palsu. Ini menyemak bentuk penaakulan secara berasingan daripada kebenaran sebenar premis yang diberi. | From “all multiples of 14 are multiples of 7” and “42 is a multiple of 14”, the conclusion “42 is a multiple of 7” follows validly. |
| Invalid argument (hujah tidak sah) | The premises can all be true while the conclusion is false. The reasoning therefore does not guarantee its conclusion, even if the conclusion happens to be true in another case. | Semua premis boleh benar sedangkan kesimpulan palsu. Oleh itu penaakulan tidak menjamin kesimpulannya, walaupun kesimpulan mungkin benar dalam kes lain. | “All multiples of 14 are multiples of 7; 21 is a multiple of 7; therefore 21 is a multiple of 14” has true premises and a false conclusion. |
| Sound argument (hujah munasabah) | A valid argument whose premises are all true. Check both validity and premise truth; with both established, the conclusion is true too. | Hujah yang sah dan semua premisnya benar. Semak kesahan dan kebenaran premis; apabila kedua-duanya dipenuhi, kesimpulan juga benar. | “All multiples of 14 are multiples of 7; 42 is a multiple of 14; therefore 42 is a multiple of 7” is sound: the form is valid and both premises are true. |
| Sufficient condition (syarat mencukupi) | A condition that guarantees the stated result when it holds. Other conditions may also lead to that result. | Syarat yang menjamin hasil yang dinyatakan apabila dipenuhi. Syarat lain mungkin turut menghasilkan keputusan itu. | Being a multiple of 14 guarantees being a multiple of 7; 21 is also a multiple of 7 even though it is not a multiple of 14. |
| Modus ponens (gunakan syarat untuk memperoleh akibat) | Use “if p then q” together with p to conclude q. First confirm the rule’s condition; then apply its result. | Gunakan “jika p maka q” bersama p untuk menyimpulkan q. Sahkan syarat peraturan dahulu, kemudian gunakan akibatnya. | If a number is a multiple of 14, it is a multiple of 7. Since 42 is a multiple of 14, conclude that 42 is a multiple of 7. |
| Modus tollens (nafikan akibat untuk menolak syarat) | Use “if p then q” and “not q” to conclude “not p”. If the required result fails, the rule’s condition cannot have held. | Gunakan “jika p maka q” dan “bukan q” untuk menyimpulkan “bukan p”. Jika akibat yang diwajibkan gagal, syarat peraturan itu tidak mungkin dipenuhi. | If a number is a multiple of 14, it is a multiple of 7. Since 25 is not a multiple of 7, conclude that it is not a multiple of 14. |
| Affirming the consequent (kesilapan menyimpulkan syarat daripada akibat) | The invalid pattern “if p then q; q; therefore p”. The same result q might occur without p, so the reverse rule must not be assumed. | Bentuk tidak sah “jika p maka q; q; maka p”. Akibat q boleh berlaku tanpa p, maka peraturan akas tidak boleh diandaikan. | Being a multiple of 7 does not prove that 21 is a multiple of 14, even though every multiple of 14 is a multiple of 7. |
| Denying the antecedent (kesilapan menolak akibat apabila syarat gagal) | The invalid pattern “if p then q; not p; therefore not q”. Failing this particular condition does not rule out another way for q to hold. | Bentuk tidak sah “jika p maka q; bukan p; maka bukan q”. Kegagalan syarat tertentu ini tidak menolak cara lain yang membolehkan q dipenuhi. | 21 is not a multiple of 14, but it is still a multiple of 7. Rejecting the first condition does not reject the result. |
| Deductive reasoning (penaakulan deduktif) | Apply given premises or a rule to obtain a conclusion that must follow if those premises are true and the reasoning is valid. | Gunakan premis atau peraturan yang diberi untuk memperoleh kesimpulan yang mesti mengikutinya apabila premis benar dan penaakulan sah. | Apply “every multiple of 14 is a multiple of 7” to the specific number 98. |
| Inductive reasoning (penaakulan induktif) | Use observed examples to suggest a pattern, prediction or wider claim. The observations may support the suggestion without guaranteeing it. | Gunakan contoh yang diperhatikan untuk mencadangkan pola, ramalan atau dakwaan lebih luas. Pemerhatian boleh menyokong cadangan itu tanpa menjaminnya. | From 4, 9, 14 and 19, predict 24 by continuing the observed increase of 5; this is a suggested continuation. |
| Generalisation (pengitlakan) | A claim extended from checked examples to a wider group or unchecked cases. Its strength depends on how well the examples support that wider scope. | Dakwaan yang diperluas daripada contoh diperiksa kepada kumpulan lebih luas atau kes belum diperiksa. Kekuatannya bergantung pada sejauh mana contoh menyokong skop lebih luas itu. | After checking 5 purple cards from a 31-card example collection, claiming that all 31 are purple goes beyond those observations. |
| Proof (bukti) | A logical justification that establishes a claim for every case in its scope using accepted facts and valid reasoning. A few matching examples alone do not prove a claim about all cases. | Justifikasi logik yang menetapkan dakwaan bagi setiap kes dalam skopnya menggunakan fakta diterima dan penaakulan sah. Beberapa contoh yang sepadan sahaja tidak membuktikan dakwaan bagi semua kes. | If n is even, write n = 2k for an integer k. Then n + 2 = 2(k + 1), so n + 2 is even too. |
| Assume / given (andaian / diberi) | Treat the information explicitly stated in the question as the starting facts for that question. Add no unstated card, property or rule. | Terima maklumat yang dinyatakan secara jelas dalam soalan sebagai fakta awal soalan itu. Jangan tambah kad, sifat atau peraturan yang tidak diberi. | If an example says “each card has exactly one category”, apply that rule to the example rather than assuming a second category. |
| Sample (sampel) | The selected cases checked from a larger target group. The sample is only the checked part, not automatically the whole group. | Kes yang dipilih dan diperiksa daripada kumpulan sasaran yang lebih besar. Sampel hanya bahagian yang diperiksa, bukan keseluruhan kumpulan secara automatik. | Inspecting 6 cards from a 31-card example collection gives a sample of those 6 selected cards. |
| Random sample (sampel rawak) | Random selection uses chance rather than picking only preferred cases. A random sample is a group of cases selected this way; its selection method must fit the whole target group. | Pemilihan rawak menggunakan peluang, bukan hanya memilih kes yang disukai. Sampel rawak ialah kumpulan kes yang dipilih melalui cara ini; kaedah pemilihannya mesti sesuai dengan seluruh kumpulan sasaran. | To inspect an example 31-card collection, shuffle all 31 card IDs and take 6 without choosing them by colour. |
| Population / target group (populasi / kumpulan sasaran) | The complete group that the study or general claim concerns. Decide its scope before selecting evidence. | Seluruh kumpulan yang menjadi tumpuan kajian atau dakwaan umum. Tentukan skopnya sebelum memilih bukti. | For a claim about every card in an example collection of 31 cards, all 31 cards form the population. |
| Sample size (saiz sampel) | The number of cases in a sample. In these evidence comparisons, count the different cards or rooms checked: repeatedly inspecting one case does not cover more different cases. | Bilangan kes dalam sampel. Dalam perbandingan bukti ini, kira kad atau bilik berbeza yang diperiksa: memeriksa satu kes berulang kali tidak meliputi lebih banyak kes berbeza. | Checking 6 different example cards covers 6 cases; checking one card 6 times still covers only one different card. |
| Selection bias (bias pemilihan) | A selection method that systematically favours some cases, making the sample unrepresentative of the intended group. A larger biased sample can still mislead. | Kaedah pemilihan yang cenderung secara sistematik kepada kes tertentu sehingga sampel tidak mewakili kumpulan sasaran. Sampel berat sebelah yang lebih besar masih boleh mengelirukan. | Choosing only cards already known to be purple gives biased evidence for a claim that all 31 example cards are purple. |
| Observation (pemerhatian) | A result actually checked or recorded. Separate what was observed from guesses about cases not yet checked. | Keputusan yang benar-benar diperiksa atau direkodkan. Bezakan perkara yang diperhatikan daripada tekaan tentang kes belum diperiksa. | “I inspected example card t41 and saw a purple mark” is an observation about t41. |
| Prediction (ramalan) | A claim about a result not yet checked. Earlier observations may support it, but do not make it certain by themselves. | Dakwaan tentang keputusan yang belum diperiksa. Pemerhatian terdahulu boleh menyokongnya tetapi tidak menjadikannya pasti dengan sendiri. | “The uninspected example card t55 will be purple” is a prediction, not an observation of t55. |
| Sequence / term (jujukan / sebutan) | A sequence is an ordered list. Each entry is a term, identified by its position. A few terms do not always determine a unique continuation. | Jujukan ialah senarai mengikut turutan. Setiap item ialah sebutan yang dikenal pasti melalui kedudukannya. Beberapa sebutan tidak semestinya menentukan satu sambungan unik. | In 6, 13, 20, 27, the third term is 20. |
| Pattern (pola) | A regularity noticed in the stated examples. It can suggest a rule, but more than one rule may fit a short list. | Aturan yang diperhatikan dalam contoh yang diberi. Ia boleh mencadangkan peraturan, tetapi lebih daripada satu peraturan mungkin sepadan dengan senarai pendek. | The list 6, 13, 20, 27 increases by 7 each time; continuing that pattern suggests 34. |
| Teaching pool (koleksi contoh dalam soalan) | The fixed example collection supplied by the question. It is not automatically the player’s actual draw pile, deck or drop rate. In bag examples, a rune means a coloured counter. | Koleksi contoh tetap yang diberi dalam soalan. Ia bukan semestinya timbunan cabutan, dek atau kadar ganjaran sebenar pemain. Dalam contoh beg, rune bermaksud pembilang berwarna. | If an example lists only {t61,t62,t63}, use those three cards even if a real game deck contains other cards. |
| Draw / select (cabut / ambil / pilih) | Take or choose one item from the stated collection. A draw does not mean drawing a picture. Check whether the item is returned before the next draw. | Ambil, cabut atau pilih satu item daripada koleksi yang diberi. Draw di sini bukan bermaksud melukis. Semak sama ada item dipulangkan sebelum cabutan seterusnya. | “Draw twice without replacement” means take one example counter, keep it out, then take another from what remains. |
| Distinct / unique card ID (ID kad yang berbeza / unik) | Each ID identifies one separate item. Different IDs remain different items even when every other stated property is the same. | Setiap ID mengenal pasti satu item berasingan. ID berlainan tetap merujuk kepada item berlainan walaupun semua sifat lain yang dinyatakan sama. | Example cards t61 and t62 are two elements of an ID set even if both are Neutral cards. |
| Category (kategori) | A group label assigned as the question describes. When each item has exactly one category, those categories cannot overlap for one item; other marks may still overlap if stated. | Label kumpulan yang ditetapkan menurut soalan. Apabila setiap item mempunyai tepat satu kategori, kategori itu tidak boleh bertindih bagi satu item; tanda lain masih boleh bertindih jika dinyatakan. | In a one-category example, a card labelled Skill is not also Attack, but it may separately have a stated circle mark. |
| Energy / cost (Energy / kos) | The stated amount of Energy needed to play a card. In a set or logic question, cost is a given card property; use that number without guessing a different cost or effect. | Jumlah Energy yang dinyatakan diperlukan untuk memainkan kad. Dalam soalan set atau logik, kos ialah sifat kad yang diberi; gunakan nombor itu tanpa meneka kos atau kesan lain. | An example card with stated cost 4 belongs to the cost-4 group, regardless of its separately stated colour. |
| Upgraded (ditandakan telah ditambah baik) | A label identifying the listed improved items. For a set question, membership comes only from the stated IDs or condition; no damage bonus, cost change or extra member is assumed. | Label yang mengenal pasti item ditambah baik yang disenaraikan. Dalam soalan set, keahlian hanya mengikut ID atau syarat yang diberi; jangan andaikan bonus serangan, perubahan kos atau ahli tambahan. | If an example gives the upgraded set G = {t71,t74}, t71 is in G and t72 is outside G unless more information is supplied. |
